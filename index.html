Specification

MCCP edition 2.0.0 distils the declared meaning-space into one artifact any system can ingest. It does not ask the reader to believe the theology in order to parse the file. belief_alignment_required is false.

A cell is a state vector: fingerprint, embedding, domain, time, abstraction, weight, edges. The cube is a 3D projection of that table. Hashes in this seed edition are author-declared partial tokens. They are not verified digests of unseen transcripts. That honesty is the chain of custody.

Full encoding rules, the cell register, and the succession note live in SPEC.md.
